About PawPals

A one-stop home for happy, healthy pets

We started as a tiny neighborhood pet shop and grew into a full pet care center. Today our team of groomers, vets and pet lovers looks after thousands of dogs, cats and small pets every year.

Every product on our shelves is chosen by pet parents like you, and every service is done with patience, gentle hands and a lot of treats.

FAQ

Good questions

Do I need an appointment for grooming?

Appointments are recommended, but walk-ins are welcome when we have space.

Which pets do you accept?

Dogs and cats for grooming, check-ups and boarding. Our shop also supplies birds, fish and small pets.

Are vaccinations required for boarding?

Yes. Please bring your pet's up-to-date vaccination record.

Do you offer home delivery for pet food?

Yes, we deliver locally. Ask us about our subscription plan.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, major cards and popular e-wallets.
